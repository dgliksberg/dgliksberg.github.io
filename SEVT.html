Solar Electric Vehicle Rear Suspension

As subteam co-lead, I designed and conducted materials testing for a trailing suspension arm for a solar-powered electric vehicle.

  • Back

Ideation

We started the design process by considering two possible arm designs, which we called the L-Arm and the Lambda-Arm. The Lambda-Arm was the design used for the previous car, and our initial goal was to determine which arm design would give better performance in terms of force transmitted into the chassis of the car. We used MATLAB to keep track of our calculatons, and to prevent re-solving everything by hand if we decided to change an assumption or design requirement.
