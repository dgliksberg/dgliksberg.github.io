Machined Sensor Mount and Bearing Holders

In January 2025, I took a short internship to remake some 3D printed components for a predictive maintenance testbed in aluminum.

Machining

I primarily used a mill for the torque sensor mount and a waterjet for the bearing mounts. I had to do some fine tuning to get the bearing mounts to come out of the waterjet ready for finishing on the mill, and some fine-tuning to get a good surface finish on the torque sensor with a face mill.
