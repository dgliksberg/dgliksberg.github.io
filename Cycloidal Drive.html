3D Printed Cycloidal Drive

In the summer of 2024, I designed and manufactured my own version of a functional Cycloidal Drive. This was partly an exercise in designing a precise mechanism with 3D printing in mind, but I also plan to use this design in future projects.

Design

I designed my cycloidal drive around off-the-shelf dowel pins that I wanted to use as rollers. I found a very helpful article that provided equations to create the cycloidal disks for the drive. I then designed a housing around the disks which fully constrains the motion of the gearbox when attached to a motor. I ensured that there was no plastic-on-plastic sliding contact to increase the lifespan of the drive.

Manufacturing and Assembly

While the off-the-shelf components were in the mail, I prepared the 3D-printed components for printing and fine-tuned my print settings for a specific makerspace printer. Once the off-the-shelf parts arrived and every custom component was printed, I was able to assemble the drive and control electronics without issues.
